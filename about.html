OUR CRAFT

The 4-Step Artisanal Journey

How every single bottle of AFEEM EDP is meticulously crafted from global flower fields to your dressing table.

01

Ethical Raw Sourcing

We source cold-pressed Italian Bergamot, Grasse Rose & Jasmine, and rich Assam Oud directly from ethical sustainable growers.

02

90-Day Oil Aging

French master perfumers age essential raw oils for 90 days in temperature-controlled dark cellars to unlock deep olfactory harmony.

03

25%-30% EDP Ratio

Formulated at extra-high Eau de Parfum concentration (25%-30%) to ensure 24-hour long-lasting scent projection in humid climates.

04

Artisanal Bottling

Bottled in heavy UV-protected crystal glass with micro-mist atomizers and hand-inspected before golden luxury sealing.
