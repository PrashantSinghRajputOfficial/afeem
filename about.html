About AFEEM

French Artistry Meets Contemporary Indian Soul

Founded with a vision to redefine the premium fragrance experience in India, AFEEM merges legacy perfumery techniques from Grasse, France with vibrant modern scent profiles tailored for the contemporary Indian lifestyle.

We believe that a fragrance is more than just a scent. It's a silent communicator, an extension of your character, and the beginning of memorable conversations. That is why every single spray is crafted to last, linger, and captivate.
